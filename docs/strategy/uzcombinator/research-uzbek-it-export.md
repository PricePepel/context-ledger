# Uzbekistan beachhead research: Ledger Handover (as of 2026-11-06)

**Bottom line:** Uzbekistan is a good place to start and to prove the product, but it is too small to be the whole market. The real fit pool is about 50–100 agencies (estimate, UNVERIFIED). At $149/mo that caps local revenue at roughly $90k–$180k a year. The UzCombinator pitch should be "prove it in Uzbekistan, then sell across Central Asia, the Caucasus and Eastern Europe". Exporting firms there have the same problem of proving their work to clients in the US and EU.

## 1. IT Park residents
- **Total residents:** 2,846 at end of May 2025 ([Daryo](https://daryo.uz/en/2025/06/07/636-new-companies-join-uzbekistans-it-park-since-start-of-2025)). Another source says "over 2,800, including 752 foreign" ([Anhor](https://anhor.uz/news/it-2/)). I found no official 2026 total (UNVERIFIED). A figure of "4,000+ firms" appears only in a snippet ([OutsourceAccelerator](https://www.outsourceaccelerator.com/?p=163348)) and is UNVERIFIED.
- **Exporters:** 1,050, of which 315 are outside Tashkent (Aug 2026) ([IT Park](https://www.it-park.uz/en/itpark/news/it-park-members-export-geography-reaches-102-countries)).
- **Foreign-capital residents:** 1,095 (Jul 2026). In H1 2026, 608 new export-oriented members joined (+178% YoY). New members split 76% IT services, 19% BPO/KPO ([IT Park](https://www.it-park.uz/en/itpark/news/it-park-uzbekistan-continues-to-strengthen-uzbekistan-s-position-as-a-global-it-destination)).
- **Employees:** about 38,600 at end-2024 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IT_Park_Uzbekistan), citing IT Park). That works out to roughly 14 staff per resident on average (my calculation).

## 4. Agency size distribution and engagement models
- **Size distribution:** no published headcount breakdown exists (UNVERIFIED).
- **Clutch sample:** of the ~33 Uzbekistan developers on Clutch's list, most are 10–49 staff at $25–49/hr. A few are 250+ (Itransition, Elinext, Qulix, Civitta) ([Clutch](https://clutch.co/uz/developers)).
- **Export mix, 2023:** outsourcing 57%, own products 23%, BPO 20% ([UzDaily](https://uzdaily.ru/en/it-park-uzbekistan-summed-up-its-work-for-2023)).
- **Export mix, later (undated):** IT services 53%, BPO 28%, gamedev 11%, products 8% ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IT_Park_Uzbekistan)).
- **Fixed-price vs staff augmentation:** no data on the split (UNVERIFIED). Policy pushes toward staff augmentation and direct hiring:
  - IT Park's "Zero Risk" program pays up to $500 per hire and up to 15% of payroll. Some clients prefer to hire developers directly rather than outsource whole projects ([IT Park, Dec 2025](https://www.outsource.gov.uz/en/media/why-the-world-is-ready-to-work-with-uzbek-engineers-the-case-of-truss-and-the-zero-risk-program)).
  - Clutch has a dedicated staff augmentation category for Uzbekistan ([Clutch](https://clutch.co/uz/it-services/staff-augmentation)).
- **Export quotas:** large residents had to export at least 10% from Oct 2024 ([Daryo](https://daryo.uz/en/2024/05/31/uzbekistans-it-industry-adopts-new-export-requirements)). The quota rises to 20% in 2026, 35% in 2027 and 50% in 2028 ([intellinews](https://www.intellinews.com/uzbekistan-mandates-export-targets-for-it-park-residents-327704)). This structurally pushes firms to win and keep foreign clients.

## 5. AI coding tool adoption
- **General population:** 7.2% use AI regularly, ranking 142nd of 147 countries (Microsoft study, 2026) ([Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2026/06/01/kazakhstan-leads-central-asia-in-ai-adoption-as-uzbekistan-struggles-with-low-usage-rates-0fddc4)).
- **Employees:** 64% say they use AI at work, mostly ChatGPT (HeadHunter survey, Jun 2025) ([Kursiv](https://uz.kursiv.media/?p=1769075)).
- **Developers:** no survey of Uzbek developers or Claude Code/Codex usage found (UNVERIFIED). Low adoption is a risk: the ideal customer must already run agent sessions. Validate this in customer interviews.

## 6. Language, timezone and trust barriers
- **Timezone:** Uzbekistan is UTC+5 (no URL fetched). That gives good overlap with the EU and about 10 hours' difference from US Eastern time.
- **Language:** one Clutch review aggregate mentions communication problems from language nuance, but for one vendor only ([Clutch](https://clutch.co/uz/it-services/system-integrators)). There is no country-level data on English proficiency (UNVERIFIED).
- **Operating problems** raised by member companies include banking, tax and hiring senior staff ([IT Park](https://www.outsource.gov.uz/de/media/it-park-uzbekistan-strengthens-cooperation-with-member-companies-and-supports-business-scaling)). Local firms also lack senior project and product managers ([Spot](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2024/02/06/u-bss-)). These gaps are exactly what weakens handover.
- **Trust signals:** IT Park itself holds ISO 27001, 9001 and 37001 ([IT Park](https://www.outsource.gov.uz/en/media/it-park-uzbekistan-attains-international-iso-90012015-certification)). I found no evidence that agency SOC 2 or ISO audits are subsidised (UNVERIFIED).

## 7. Evidence that clients need proof of quality or provenance
- **Uzbekistan-specific:** none found (UNVERIFIED). This is the biggest gap to close with interviews.
- **Indirect, from the client side:**
  - Development contracts increasingly carry AI-generated-code disclosure and warranty clauses ([Mike Young Law](https://mikeyounglaw.com/should-your-software-development-agreement-disclose-ai-written-code/); [ThoughtRiver](https://www.thoughtriver.com/resources/ai-clauses-in-commercial-contracts-a-practical-guide-part-1)).
  - Buyer-side forms assign all AI outputs, including code, to the customer ([Taylor English](https://insights.taylorenglish.com/post/102k73v/integrating-ai-into-your-services-heres-what-to-include-in-your-customer-contra)).
  - A law-firm guide on whether offshore engineers in Armenia, a comparable post-Soviet outsourcing hub, may use AI tools on client projects ([armenian-lawyer.com](https://armenian-lawyer.com/business-immigration/armenian-engineers-ai-tools-customer-contract/)).
- **Implication:** sell Ledger Handover as the agency's "AI-use disclosure and evidence" attachment to the contract.

## 8. Beachhead estimate (my model, UNVERIFIED)
1,050 exporters
→ about 60% non-BPO IT services ≈ 630
→ about 60% with 3–30 staff ≈ 380
→ about 45% doing fixed-price or project work ≈ 170
→ about 40% with enterprise or regulated foreign clients ≈ 70
→ about 50% already using Claude Code or Codex ≈ **35 today, 50–100 by 2027**.
