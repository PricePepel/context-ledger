## Program format
- 45 days, in person in Tashkent (Alisher Navoiy 11A). It runs as six weeks with one goal per week ([FAQ](https://uzcombinator.uz/en/faq)).
- Each team gets a weekly mentor, a shared office and a **$1,000 stipend** that is not repaid ([FAQ](https://uzcombinator.uz/en/faq)).
- Season 3 adds free meals and more than $60K in tokens and credits, and pitches "up to $1M" at Demo Day ([S3 announcement](https://uzcombinator.uz/en/news/uzcombinator-45-kunlik-akseleratsiya-dasturining-3-mavsumi-rasman-ochildi)).
- The program is free to join ([gov.uz](https://gov.uz/oz/yoshlar/news/view/193066)).
- The stated aim for Season 3 is to turn startups into revenue-generating companies ([S3 announcement](https://uzcombinator.uz/en/news/uzcombinator-45-kunlik-akseleratsiya-dasturining-3-mavsumi-rasman-ochildi)).
- Founders Camps run in the regions between batches ([FAQ](https://uzcombinator.uz/en/faq)).

## Eligibility
- **Stage:** idea or first product ([FAQ](https://uzcombinator.uz/en/faq)).
- **Age:** no limit stated ([FAQ](https://uzcombinator.uz/en/faq)).
- **Team:** no fixed size. Teams of 2–3 are preferred, and solo founders are "harder" ([FAQ](https://uzcombinator.uz/en/faq)).
- **Region:** startups from every region of Uzbekistan can apply ([FAQ](https://uzcombinator.uz/en/faq)).
- **Incorporation:** not required. Many teams form an LLC only once payments start ([FAQ](https://uzcombinator.uz/en/faq)).
- **Citizenship and residency:** not stated (UNVERIFIED).
  - The main Demo Day investor, Yoshlar Ventures, backs "Uzbek founders" wherever they incorporate ([the-tech.kz](https://the-tech.kz/?p=42323), [UzDaily](https://www.uzdaily.uz/en/yoshlar-ventures-doubles-fund-to-us20m-to-support-startups/)).
  - Being physically in Tashkent for 45 days is effectively required.

## Investment terms
- **Equity:** UzCombinator takes a stake, but the size is **negotiated per startup at the interview**. No percentage is published ([FAQ](https://uzcombinator.uz/en/faq)).
- **SAFE:** not confirmed. The FAQ says SAFEs are usually used through a foreign holding company because Uzbek LLC law is not fully adapted to them ([FAQ](https://uzcombinator.uz/en/faq)).
- **Directory range:** awards.gov.uz lists investments of $40K–$400K ([awards.gov.uz](https://awards.gov.uz/en/ecosystem/accelerators-directory)). This is UNVERIFIED as UzCombinator's own cheque.

## Related programs to combine with
- **IT Park residency** ([IT Park, Jan 2026](https://www.it-park.uz/index.php/en/itpark/news/tax-incentives-for-startups-and-foreign-investors-in-uzbekistan-s-it-sector)):
  - 0% corporate income tax, VAT, social tax and turnover tax until 1 January 2028.
  - 7.5% personal income tax on employee wages, against 12% for non-residents.
  - 5% tax on dividends to foreign shareholders until 2040.
  - Firms earning more than 50% of revenue from exports pay no tax except VAT from 2028 to 2040 (Decree No. 157, [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_uz/technical/tax-alerts/2024/10/ey-uz-decree-on-additional-incentives-to-support-it-park-residents)).
  - In July 2026 the extension to 2040 was still reported as "under consideration" ([kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2026/05/23/how-much-startup-funding-can-you-raise-in-uzbekistan-693a5e)). Treat the post-2028 regime as UNVERIFIED.
  - Selling Ledger Handover to foreign agencies would qualify the company as export-oriented.
- **IT Park "Zero-Risk":** up to 12 months of free regional office space, a payroll subsidy of 5–15%, and up to 50% of staff training costs reimbursed ([IT Park](https://www.it-park.uz/index.php/en/itpark/news/tax-incentives-for-startups-and-foreign-investors-in-uzbekistan-s-it-sector)).
- **IT Visa:** 3 years, open to founders of IT Park resident companies ([kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2026/03/11/uzbekistan-expands-eligibility-and-requirements-for-it-visas)).
- **Yoshlar Ventures:** a $20M fund, targeting $30M by 2027, investing at pre-seed and seed, with cheques up to $400K ([UzDaily](https://www.uzdaily.uz/en/yoshlar-ventures-doubles-fund-to-us20m-to-support-startups/)). The median deal is $30K ([kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2026/05/23/how-much-startup-funding-can-you-raise-in-uzbekistan-693a5e)).
- **IT Park Ventures:** $30M fund, Seed and Series A, cheques up to $3M ([UzDaily](https://uzdaily.uz/en/it-park-ventures-expands-fund-to-us30-million/)). Pre-seed median is $50K ([kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2026/05/23/how-much-startup-funding-can-you-raise-in-uzbekistan-693a5e)).
- **UzVC:**
  - It is a fund-of-funds ([kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2026/05/23/how-much-startup-funding-can-you-raise-in-uzbekistan-693a5e)).
  - Its pre-seed median is $100K ([kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2026/05/23/how-much-startup-funding-can-you-raise-in-uzbekistan-693a5e)).
  - Its new "Kelajak tadbirkori" program (2027–2030) offers up to 5 billion so'm per startup as a convertible loan or SAFE. The state stake is capped at 49%, and up to 30 startups are funded ([infoedu](https://infoedu.uz/startap-5-mlrd-garovsiz-foizsiz-kelajak-tadbirkori)).
- **Youth business resolution (14 February 2025):** equity investment of up to 2 billion UZS for export-oriented graduates of an acceleration program. This is the natural follow-on after UzCombinator ([qalampir](https://qalampir.uz/en/news/khorizh-bozoriga-chik-uvchi-startap-loyi%D2%B3alar-2-mlrd-sumgacha-mablag-olishi-mumkin-114616)).
- **Startup Garage:** an incubator in Tashkent and the regions, registration at startupgarage.uz. IT Park Ventures can invest directly in its residents ([daryo](https://daryo.uz/en/2025/06/26/uzbekistan-strengthens-startup-ecosystem-as-it-park-ventures-partners-with-startup-garage)).

## Application checklist
1. Submit before **10 November 2026, 23:59**. Sign in at uzcombinator.uz/apply with Google or email ([apply](https://uzcombinator.uz/en/apply/home)).
2. The form fields are behind the login, so they are UNVERIFIED. Expect these:
   - founder names, roles, Uzbek ID or citizenship details, and phone/Telegram
   - a description of the product in your own words (no deck needed)
   - your stage, a demo link and the GitHub repo
3. Show founder-market fit and commitment: full-time status, a co-founder if possible, and 45 days available in Tashkent.
4. Bring evidence of demand. The Yurideks bar was 30 or more users interviewed:
   - interviews with agency owners
   - letters of intent or pilot agreements
   - one paid or committed packet
   - a sample signed packet built from synthetic transcripts
5. Prepare a one-line answer that maps the product to the focus areas: local AI governance for Uzbekistan's IT export sector.
6. Have the incorporation plan ready: a Tashkent LLC that becomes an IT Park resident, with an optional foreign holding company for SAFEs.
7. Prepare for the 30-minute interview: your target equity range and use of funds against a cheque of $40K–$150K.
8. Have a 3-minute Demo Day pitch drafted and a list of follow-on funds: Yoshlar, Aloqa, SQB, IT Park Ventures and UzVC.
